Encoder vs Decoder in Transformers

The reader and the writer behind every LLM

sourceAmit Shekhar · Outcome School · Aug 2026

A Transformer has two building blocks. The Encoder understands the input. The Decoder writes the output. This page shows how they differ.

A

The Big Picture

A Transformer is the architecture behind most modern language AI. Tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all use it.

The one big difference

The Encoder reads and understands the whole input at once. The Decoder writes new output one token at a time, looking only at the past.

B

The Encoder: It Understands

The Encoder reads all tokens together. It looks left and right. This is bidirectional reading.

Self-attention lets every token see every other token. Later words help explain earlier ones. In "The bank of the river was muddy", the word "river" disambiguates "bank".

The Encoder builds a deep meaning for each token. It writes nothing new. It only understands.

C

The Decoder: It Writes

The Decoder writes one token at a time, from left to right. Future tokens do not exist yet. Causal masking hides them.

Each new token depends only on the past tokens. This is autoregressive generation. The Decoder is like a writer building a sentence word by word.

D

Reader vs Writer

Encoderthe reader
  • Reads the whole input at once
  • Looks in both directions
  • Builds deep understanding
  • Writes nothing new
Decoderthe writer
  • Writes output token by token
  • Looks only backward
  • Uses past tokens only
  • Generates new text

Remember only this line. The Encoder is a reader that looks in both directions. The Decoder is a writer that looks only backward. That is the heart of the difference.

E

Three Types of Transformers

Encoder-onlypure understanding
  • Example: BERT
  • Tasks: sentiment, embeddings for search
Decoder-onlypure generation
  • Example: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini
  • Tasks: chat, writing
Encoder-Decoderunderstand, then generate
  • Example: T5
  • Tasks: translation, summarization
F

Translation Flow: "I love AI" → "J'aime l'IA"

The Encoder understands the English sentence fully. The Decoder writes the French output step by step.

Cross-attention is the bridge. It carries the Encoder's understanding into the Decoder.

Why chat models are decoder-only

A good writer must also understand well. A large Decoder learns to do both at the same time. That is why decoder-only models power most chat tools today.
